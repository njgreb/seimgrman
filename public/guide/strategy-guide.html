PHASE 1 ▪ THE ALL-HANDS MACHINE

WEAK TO: TICKET SPLIT

The armor deflects everything. Only the cockpit takes damage, and only while its shutter is up between attacks — it sits at jump-shot height, so jump and fire into the gap the moment it opens.

MANDATORY ALL-HANDS ▪ BACKLOG DUMP ▪ 5-YEAR ROADMAP ▪ EXEC ESCALATION

PHASE 2 ▪ THE ESCAPE POD

WEAK TO: CALENDAR BLOCK

He bails out into a pod that flies, teleports and ignores gravity. Between attacks it sinks into jump-shot range to taunt you — that hover is the whole window. Switch weapons before it drops.

SKIP-LEVEL! ▪ ALL-HANDS! ▪ DOUBLE-BOOKED!
NO RETRIES AT HQ. Lose to him — or quit the fight — and the run ends REORGED and gets scored anyway. Every manager you beat still counts.
PRO TIPS
  • Seven hits, not twenty-eight. Every boss holds 28 HP. The weakness weapon takes 4 a hit; the PR BUSTER takes 1.
  • CRITICAL! is the receipt. If that word isn't flashing, you brought the wrong weapon.
  • They enrage at half HP — extra volleys, shorter gaps. The second half of a fight is not the first half.
  • Mashing is wasted. A boss is invulnerable for 1/4 second after each hit, and every trigger pull that touches nothing costs you accuracy.
  • You can't get stranded. Weapon energy refills one point every 0.7s, so the weakness weapon always comes back.
  • Spread beats spray. Accuracy counts trigger pulls that landed — a 3-way shot counts once, whether one pellet hits or three.
  • Pause is free. The fight clock stops, and intros, menus and boss select never counted.
  • Retries are not free. Time, shots and hits carry across every attempt in the run, including the ones you lost.
